## Synthesis
**Areas of Agreement**

The paper aligns with established research in emphasizing that social norm interventions—strategies that highlight what is commonly done to encourage similar behavior—can effectively promote prosocial behaviors. It reinforces the consensus that simply providing information about what others do (descriptive norms) or what others approve of (injunctive norms) can shift individual actions in prosocial directions. The research appears to recognize that these interventions work best when there is a shared cultural context where individuals interpret cues similarly.
