# Research Synthesis

## 1. Points of Agreement

Both papers converge on the recognition that digital technologies are fundamentally reshaping the landscape of business practice and research, and that existing theoretical frameworks are insufficient to capture these changes. They share a conviction that phenomena like social media's influence on consumer behavior (Paper 1) and AI's integration into management (Paper 2) represent more than incremental shifts—they constitute structural transformations requiring new conceptual tools. Both also implicitly endorse the view that technological change in business is not deterministic but contingent on how organizations, managers, and researchers choose to engage with it. Furthermore, each paper positions itself at a moment of disciplinary reflection, calling on academic communities to develop more nuanced, forward-looking research agendas rather than merely cataloging the latest tools or platforms.
