## Synthesis
**Agreement**  
All three works converge on the idea that, as AI systems become increasingly multi‑agent, the *emergent* properties of their interactions—whether linguistic, behavioral, or coordinative—cannot be ignored. The survey on emergent language (Paper 1) emphasizes that agents trained together develop novel communication protocols that are essential for collective performance. Paper 2 (MAEBE) builds on this by arguing that those same emergent protocols introduce *new safety and alignment risks* that isolated‑system evaluations miss. Paper 3 (SPEAR) demonstrates that, in a concrete domain (smart‑contract auditing), emergent coordination is not merely a side‑effect but a *design requirement*; without it the system fails to cover all attack surfaces.
