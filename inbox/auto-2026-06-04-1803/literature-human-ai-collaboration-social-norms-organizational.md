**1. Points of Agreement**

Both papers share an underlying conviction that digital technologies are not merely operational tools but forces that fundamentally reshape organizational behavior and consumer–company relationships. Paper 1 frames the internet and social media as having *changed* consumer behavior and business conduct, while Paper 2 positions AI as generating a similar — and arguably more profound — restructuring of management roles. They also appear to agree on a *research-agenda orientation*: both seem to be framing pieces (a "perspectives" piece and a conceptual exploration of a paradox) rather than empirical studies, suggesting consensus that the field needs conceptual scaffolding before further empirical accumulation. Implicitly, both treat technology as a domain where the gap between practice and scholarly understanding is widening fast.
