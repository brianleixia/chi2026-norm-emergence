**What They Agree On**

This paper contributes to a growing body of evidence suggesting that traditional social norm interventions—particularly those highlighting what "most people do"—often fail to achieve lasting prosocial behavior change. The core finding that effectiveness diminishes in heterogeneous populations aligns with a fundamental challenge in social psychology: when people lack shared referential frameworks, norm-based messaging loses its persuasive power.
