## Synthesis
Based on the provided excerpts, here is a synthesis of the two papers:

**1. Points of Agreement**
Both papers agree that the proliferation of autonomous, interacting AI agents fundamentally transforms the security and governance landscape, rendering traditional frameworks insufficient. They share a core concern that as agents operate within complex sociotechnical systems—negotiating with other agents, humans, and digital environments—the boundaries of accountability and control become blurred. Consequently, both works call for new, holistic design philosophies. Rather than treating security, safety, and governance as add-on features to be implemented post-hoc, they advocate for frameworks where these properties are integrated into the system architecture from the earliest stages of development to manage emergent multi-agent risks.

**2. Points of Disagreement**
While both papers address the challenges of multi-agent AI, they appear to prioritize different sources of risk and governance mechanisms. The first paper (Open Challenges in Multi-Agent Security) frames the problem primarily through the lens of adversarial security, focusing on the vulnerabilities that arise when AI agents interact directly with untrusted entities across open internet platforms and physical environments. In contrast, the second paper (Integrated Design and Governance of Agentic AI Systems) places a heavier emphasis on *adaptive information governance* and sociotechnical coordination, suggesting that the primary challenge is managing the flow of information and aligning complex human-AI collaborations, rather than just securing against external threats.

**3. Most Urgent Open Question**
The most urgent open question bridging these two perspectives is: **How can we design governance protocols that are robust enough to ensure security against adversarial multi-agent threats, yet flexible enough to enable the fluid, adaptive information exchange required for human-AI collaboration in open environments?** Resolving this tension is critical before agentic AI systems can be safely deployed at scale.
